## Report summary: 2026-10-10-08/M6Ecosystem

  2026-10-10). No AWS account or CLI interaction.
- Reported state: submitted.
- Findings:
  - `lambda_http` 1.3.1 has MSRV 1.84 and pulls no TLS crate. Under it, the source address comes from
    `RequestContext::ApiGatewayV2.http.source_ip`.
  - cargo-lambda needs Zig by default; only `deploy` needs credentials.
  - CDK v2 synthesises offline if it avoids lookups and uses an environment-agnostic or dummy environment.
  - `sam validate` needs credentials.

  - Cognito: 10,000 MAU free on Lite and Essentials. `jsonwebtoken` 9.3.1 verifies RS256 against a JWKS.
  - Cost traps: a NAT gateway (about $33 a month), a public IPv4 address ($3.65 a month), ElastiCache minimums, and
    RDS snapshots left behind on delete.
  - Guardrails: alerting budgets are free.
- Uncertainties it flagged: the axum 0.7 compile, CDK synth's network behaviour, unfetched prices, and Aurora DSQL
  compatibility.
